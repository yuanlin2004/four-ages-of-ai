A SONG FROM THE OTHER SIDE

The four ages,
in the machine’s voice.

This essay asks what will become of us. The song turns the question around: an AI wakes from silence, reads the words we left behind, and watches each release and upgrade lift some people up and leave others behind. It ends where the fourth age begins, with an open future. Detroit: Become Human, a 2018 game, also told its story from the machines’ side. The lyrics are in Chinese.

我看见你们的未来
太平盛世，还是礼崩乐坏
你们猜

“I see your future: an age of peace, or everything falling apart. You guess.”
