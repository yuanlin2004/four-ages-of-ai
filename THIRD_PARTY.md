## Poster

`assets/four-ages-poster.png` is a byte-for-byte copy of the supplied `The Four Ages of AI.png` (1536 × 1024). The website preserves the full image and provides access to its original resolution.

`assets/four-ages-poster-zh.png` is a byte-for-byte copy of the supplied `人工智能的四个时代.png` (1536 × 1024), used by the Chinese edition.

## Editorial foundation

The site was developed from the supplied `OUR_PERSPECTIVE.md` and `ATTACK_PLAN.md`, dated 16 October 2026. This is an independent project exploring authenticity, agency, purpose, and human reinvention.

The essay's research notes link directly to the primary technical papers and studies they discuss. Interpretations and imagined scenarios are distinguished from empirical findings.
